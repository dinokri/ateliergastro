# Administrator Guide — Atelier Bistro

This document outlines the day-to-day operations and configurations for restaurant management staff.

## 1. Accessing the Dashboard

1. Navigate to the top navigation header and click the **Admin** button, or append `?view=admin` to the URL.
2. Sign in with Google using an authorized account (`dinokrickovic555@gmail.com`), or use the manager quick access button for testing.

## 2. Managing Live Orders

- **New Orders Notification**: When an order arrives, it shows up under the **Narudžbe (Orders)** tab and on the **Kuhinjski Displej (KDS)**.
- **Workflow Transitions**:
  - `Nova (New)` &rarr; `Potvrdi (Confirmed)`
  - `Priprema (Cooking in kitchen)`
  - `Spremno (Ready for pickup)` or `Dostava (Out for delivery)`
  - `Uručeno (Delivered / Completed)`
- **Printing Receipts**: Click the printer icon next to any order to preview and print thermal/paper guest receipts.

## 3. Managing Table Reservations

- Access the **Rezervacije (Reservations)** tab.
- Click **Potvrdi (Confirm)** to approve a guest booking or **Odbij (Reject)** if fully booked.
- Review seating areas (Indoor Salon, Olive Terrace, Chef's Table, Wine Cellar) and special dietary notes.

## 4. Menu & Pricing Updates

- Under **Jelovnik & Cijene**:
  - One-click toggle between **Dostupno** (In Stock) and **Rasprodano** (Out of Stock).
  - Click the edit icon to change prices in BAM (KM), descriptions, ingredients, and allergen tags.
  - Click **Kreiraj Novo Jelo** to add seasonal specials.

## 5. Table QR Code Generation

- Click **QR Meni** in the header.
- Select the table number (e.g. Table #1 to #20, or Terrace tables).
- Download the high-resolution PNG or click **Printaj Karticu za Sto** to print physical table stands. When scanned by guests, the digital menu will automatically recognize their table number.

## 6. Business Settings

- Update restaurant contact info, phone number, delivery radius fee, free delivery threshold, and operating hours under **Postavke Restorana (Settings)**.
